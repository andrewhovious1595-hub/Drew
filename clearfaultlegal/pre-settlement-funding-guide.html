What Is "Non-Recourse" Funding?

Unlike personal loans or credit cards, true pre-settlement funding is completely non-recourse. The funding company purchases an equitable lien against the potential future settlement or jury verdict. If you lose your case, you owe zero dollars. Neither your personal bank accounts, your home, nor your credit score can ever be touched.
