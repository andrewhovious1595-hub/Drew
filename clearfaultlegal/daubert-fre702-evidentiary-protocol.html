The 5-Point Courtroom Survival Protocol

1. Engineer-Supplied Coordinate Lock

Never allow an animator to keyframe collision motion by eye. Every frame's spatial coordinates (X, Y, Z, pitch, roll, yaw) must be computed by an engineer using Newton's laws of motion, conservation of momentum, or calibrated crash simulation software (e.g., PC-Crash, HVE).

2. Photogrammetry & Camera Calibration

When reconstructing collisions from surveillance CCTV or dashcam footage, calibrate the virtual camera using PhotoModeler. Match utility poles, lane stripes, and permanent roadway landmarks to FARO laser scan coordinates to prove camera perspective authenticity.

3. Bosch CDR Cryptographic Hash Verification

Validate the chain of custody for black-box data using SHA-256 cryptographic hashes from the original Bosch CDR raw export file (.cdr). Confirm that ignition cycles match the incident to prevent defense accusations of testing artifact corruption.

4. Elimination of Cinematic Speculation

Avoid Hollywood clichés such as dramatic explosions, screeching sound effects, or exaggerated vehicle crumpling. Under FRE 107 and FRE 403, exhibits that inflame juror passion rather than clarify engineering calculations are prime targets for exclusion.

5. Early Disclosure & Expert Deposition Preparation

Disclose 3D simulation files, underlying coordinate spreadsheets, and software parameter assumptions well before trial. Early disclosure eliminates defense ambush claims and positions your expert witness as the most credible authority in the courtroom.
